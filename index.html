About

I'm a Seattle-based product designer with over a decade of experience designing B2B software. My recent focus has been healthcare contract negotiation tooling at Rivet Health, later acquired by Zelis — building the tools teams rely on to negotiate, model, and finalize high-stakes contracts.

Before that, I worked on the Competitiveness Opportunities product at Expedia Group, helping property partners understand and act on pricing insights.
