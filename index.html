About

I am a UX leader with a Master's degree in Human Centered Design & Engineering from the University of Washington, one of the top UX programs in the country. I later returned to the program as a part-time instructor, and I continue to teach and mentor emerging designers through my work as well as through Hexagon and ADPList.

I started my career in Fortune 500 companies but over the past six years, I've focused on product design in startup environments, where I work across the full spectrum of UX — from research and strategy to interaction and visual design. I'm drawn to early-stage and high-growth teams because they allow me to move quickly, shape product direction, and drive measurable business impact.

I am a second-generation Washingtonian and deeply connected to the Pacific Northwest. Outside of work, I enjoy cooking, bird watching, drawing, and traveling.
